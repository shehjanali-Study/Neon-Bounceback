Controls

Aim and Charge. Click and hold (or touch and drag) on your projectile, then pull backward away from your intended direction. Pulling farther back increases launch power, while rotating your cursor adjusts the firing angle.
Preview Trajectory. Watch the dotted aiming guide to line up your initial shot and predict your first bounce off the neon walls.
Launch. Release the button or lift your finger to fire the projectile across the arena.
Reset or Retry. Hit the quick-restart key or button if a shot goes off course, letting you jump right back into the action after a frustrating miss.
